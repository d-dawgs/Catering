# Pearl Island Catering Menu — Items Listed in Both Menus

**Purpose:** A locally accessible text reference for food descriptions that appear in both the 2026 sample menu and the menu currently linked from Pearl Island’s Catering page.

**How to read this:** Descriptions are transcribed and lightly cleaned from the 2026 menu. Website names are included in parentheses when they differ. A name/description match does not confirm that the item is currently available, priced, or orderable.

**Sources**
- 2026 sample menu: [official PDF](https://pearlislandcatering.com/s/2026-Pearl-Island-Catering-Menu-WOP_1_8_2026-Google-Docs-fjbj.pdf)
- Website-linked Special Events Menu: [official PDF](https://pearlislandcatering.com/s/2022-Pearl-Island-Menu.pdf), linked from [Pearl Island’s Catering page](https://pearlislandcatering.com/catering)

The website currently links the 2022 menu. Items below are included only where the 2026 sample menu has a corresponding item in that website-linked menu. Items appearing only in the 2026 PDF are omitted.

## Appetizers

- **Caribbean Crab Cakes** — Served with Caribbean sauce, lemon aioli, and fresh chives.
- **Cubano Sandos** (website: Cubano Roll-ups) — Slow-roasted pork, uncured honey ham, Creole mustard, Swiss cheese, and pickles on a toasted sweet Hawaiian roll.
- **Roasted Artichoke and Wild Mushroom Dip** (website: Roasted Artichoke and Wild Mushroom Crostini) — Marinated artichoke and garlic-infused wild mushrooms with house-made vegan garlic aioli, served with toasted crostini and raw vegetables. Gluten-free crostini can be provided on request.
- **Caribbean Chicken Wing Bites or Cauliflower Wing Bites** (website: Caribbean Chicken Wings or Cauliflower Wings) — Available in Jerk, Island Rub, Mango-Pineapple, Tamarind, or BBQ. Minimum: 25 servings.
- **Caprese Pops** (website: Caprese Lollipops) — Smoked mozzarella pearls, cherry tomatoes, fresh basil, and house balsamic reduction. Vegetarian and gluten-free.
- **Mini Sliders** (website: Slow Roasted Pork or BBQ Chicken Sliders) — Pearl Island protein on a brioche slider bun. 2026 menu options include BBQ chicken, chicken salad, curry chicken salad, pulled pork, BBQ jackfruit, and Caprese.

The 2026 menu lists a 10-serving minimum for appetizers generally; wing bites have a 25-serving minimum.

## Sides

- **Rice with Pigeon Peas** — Caribbean-seasoned rice with capers, olives, roasted red pepper, and pigeon peas.
- **Pearl Island Mac and Cheese** — Macaroni in béchamel cheese sauce with sharp cheddar.
- **Tostones** — Fried plantains.
- **Amarillos** — Ripe sweet plantains.
- **Pikliz** — Spicy Haitian slaw.
- **Rainbow Slaw** — Green and purple cabbage, rainbow peppers, red onion, fresh mango, mango chutney, and Pearl Island’s slaw dressing.
- **White Rice** — White rice.
- **Yellow Rice** — Turmeric-seasoned white rice.
- **Tropical Fried Rice** — Turmeric-stewed rice with papaya, pineapple, carrots, peas, onions, and peppers.
- **Roasted Vegetables** — Oven-roasted butternut squash, cauliflower, and Brussels sprouts with olive oil and fresh Italian herbs.

## Salads

- **Kale Salad** (website: Pearl Island Kale Salad) — Organic kale with pan-roasted mushrooms, carrots, and watermelon radish, served with Pearl Island’s shallot vinaigrette.
- **Medley Salad** (website: Pearl Island’s Rainbow Salad) — Organic mixed greens, kale, and baby spinach with rainbow peppers, English cucumbers, and heirloom tomatoes, served with Pearl Island’s shallot vinaigrette.
- **Rainbow Slaw** — Green and purple cabbage, rainbow peppers, red onion, fresh mango, mango chutney, and Pearl Island’s slaw dressing.

The Medley/Rainbow Salad match is based on the overlapping greens, cucumber, pepper, and tomato description; the name and dressing wording differ between menus.

## Caribbean entrees

The 2026 menu identifies these Caribbean entrees as gluten-free and dairy-free.

- **Curry Chicken** — Oven-roasted chicken sautéed with onions and simmered in house-made coconut curry sauce.
- **Jerk Chicken** — Oven-roasted chicken sautéed with onions and simmered in house-made six-spice jerk sauce.
- **Slow Roasted Pork** — Pearl Island’s “16 hours of Love” pork, rubbed with oregano, garlic, lemon, and culantro marinade, then slow-roasted.
- **Sous Poulet Chicken** — Haitian-inspired chicken with gravy; epis-marinated chicken simmered with onions in a sweet-heat gravy.
- **Curry Tofu** (Vegan) — Fried tofu sautéed with onions and simmered in house-made coconut curry sauce.
- **Creole Beans** (Vegan) — Slow-simmered red beans with onions, rainbow peppers, and Pearl Island Caribbean seasoning.
- **Jerk Tofu** (Vegan) — Fried tofu sautéed with onions and simmered in house-made six-spice jerk seasoning.

## Specialty entree

- **Cajun Salmon** — Salmon rubbed with olive oil and house-made Cajun seasoning, then oven-roasted until tender. (The website-linked menu describes roasted Cajun-marinated salmon with savory roasted red pepper sauce.)

## Individual hot box meals

The website-linked menu also lists hot lunch boxes containing several of the shared entrees above. The 2026 menu describes half platters with 4 oz. of protein and full platters with 6 oz. Each is served with rice with pigeon peas, kale salad, fried or sweet plantains with garlic aioli, and pikliz.

Shared hot-box proteins: Curry Chicken, Jerk Chicken, Slow Roasted Pork, Sous Poulet Chicken, Curry Tofu, Creole Beans, and Jerk Tofu.

## Cold box meals and salad lunches

### Signature sandwiches

The 2026 menu says cold box meals must be ordered at least 10 per lunchbox type. Each includes chips and a cookie or fruit.

- **Roasted Turkey** — Turkey breast seasoned with Pearl Island spice blend, lettuce, tomato, sharp cheddar, and garlic aioli.
- **Chicken Salad Sandwich** — Chopped oven-roasted chicken thighs, celery, and garlic aioli, with sharp cheddar, lettuce, and tomato.
- **Roasted Chicken Sandwich** — Pearl Island-spiced roasted chicken breast with cheddar, lettuce, tomato, and garlic aioli.
- **Barbecue Pulled Pork Sandwich** — Caribbean-seasoned slow-roasted pulled pork simmered in BBQ sauce and topped with pikliz.
- **Cubano Sandwich** — Slow-roasted pork, honey ham, Swiss cheese, Creole mustard, and pickles.

### Signature salad lunches

Both menus list Kale Salad and Rainbow Salad with a choice of marinated tofu, roasted chicken, or shrimp. The 2026 menu says these are served buffet-style; individually packaged portions cost extra. Each includes chips and a cookie or fruit, and the minimum is 10 per box type.

2026 dressing options: Shallot Vinaigrette, Zesty-Lime Vinaigrette, and Lemon-Honey Vinaigrette.

## Not included

Breakfast items, desserts, and dishes without a clear match in the website-linked menu are omitted. Confirm the live catalog, prices, availability, dietary statements, and quantity rules with Pearl Island before using this as production ordering data.
